## Web Design and Development Work

<a href="https://www.andaluzagestiona.es/" rel="dofollow">Andaluza Gestiona</a> is a company with over 25 years of experience specialising in environmental consulting, integrated waste management, and waste recovery and recycling.

Our work with Andaluza Gestiona consisted of designing and developing their website from scratch, aiming to clearly and professionally convey the services they offer and the trust built from their long track record in the environmental sector. We created a modern, fast, and easy-to-navigate website for both businesses and individuals looking for efficient, tailored waste management.

We also applied an SEO strategy focused on key terms such as "waste management", "waste recovery" and "environmental consulting", helping to improve their visibility in searches related to their industry.
